CHRONOLOGY

Timeline

This timeline presents the documented sequence of events surrounding Loretta Lynn Willoughby's disappearance, murder, investigation, court proceedings, and the continuing effort to preserve the historical record. Each entry is based upon contemporary newspaper coverage or other documented historical sources.

February 10, 1985

Loretta disappears

Fifteen-year-old Loretta Lynn Willoughby disappeared while walking to a grocery store in Irvine, Kentucky. When she failed to return home, family members, friends, and law enforcement began searching for her.

February–May 1985

Search continues

Kentucky State Police assumed the lead role in the investigation while local agencies assisted. Newspaper coverage documented continued searches, public concern, and repeated requests for information.

May 10, 1985

Loretta is found

Loretta's remains were discovered in a wooded area near Broadway in Irvine. The Kentucky State Medical Examiner later identified the remains and ruled the death a homicide.

Summer 1985

Community demands answers

Public concern remained high. Letters published in local newspapers questioned the progress of the investigation, while public officials reassured residents that the Kentucky State Police continued to actively pursue the case.

Late 1986

Grand jury indictment

Following approximately twenty months of investigation and testimony from forty-four witnesses, an Estill County grand jury indicted Mickey Reed for Loretta's murder.

January 1987

Trial postponed

Court proceedings were delayed after prosecutors requested additional time to prepare evidence, including the transcription of recorded interviews.

April 1987

Murder charge dismissed

Before trial, the indictment against Mickey Reed was dismissed after the court determined the available evidence was insufficient to proceed. No one has ever been convicted of Loretta's murder.

1987–Present

Case remains unsolved

Although the criminal case concluded, Loretta's homicide remains unsolved. The investigation has never been formally resolved.

July 2026

Larry Willoughby dies

Loretta's father, Larry Willoughby, died without seeing justice for his daughter. A retrospective newspaper article revisited the case, bringing renewed attention to the unsolved homicide more than forty years later.

Today

The historical record continues

The Loretta Willoughby Archive continues to preserve newspaper articles, photographs, public records, and other historical materials documenting Loretta's life, her murder, and the investigation that followed.
