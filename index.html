Loretta Lynn Willoughby

About the Archive

The Loretta Willoughby Archive preserves the documented history of Loretta's life, her unsolved murder, and the investigation that followed. Through newspapers, public records, photographs, and community memories, the project seeks to ensure that Loretta is remembered and that the historical record remains accessible to future generations.
