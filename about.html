About the Project

Preserving the historical record

The Loretta Willoughby Archive preserves the documented history of Loretta's life, her unsolved murder, and the investigation that followed. Through newspapers, public records, photographs, and community memories, the project seeks to ensure that Loretta is remembered and that the historical record remains accessible to future generations.

What the archive contains

  • Digitized newspaper clippings and transcripts
  • Links to full-page newspaper scans for verification
  • A searchable index organized by date, source, name, and subject
  • A documented timeline of the case
  • Historical context drawn from available public sources

Editorial principles

This project is intended to preserve and organize the available historical record. It does not present rumor, speculation, or unsupported accusations as fact.

Information is identified by source whenever possible. Newspaper reporting, public records, and personal recollections may contain errors, omissions, or conflicting accounts. Readers are encouraged to consult the original documents when conducting research.

Accuracy and corrections

OCR-generated transcripts may contain spelling, punctuation, or formatting errors. Full-page scans are provided where available so readers can review the original publication.

Dates, names, and descriptions may be revised when stronger documentation becomes available.

Independent and noncommercial

The Loretta Willoughby Archive is an independent, noncommercial historical preservation project. It is not affiliated with law enforcement, the courts, a newspaper, or any member of Loretta's family.
